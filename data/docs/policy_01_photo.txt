# Photography and Media Consent

No photograph or video of a player may be published on any club channel without a photo consent record on file for that player. Consent is recorded at registration and may be withdrawn at any time by written notice to the club secretary, taking effect within five working days. Where consent is withdrawn, existing published images featuring that player are removed from club-controlled channels. Photography by spectators at fixtures is permitted for personal use only. Coaches may use video for coaching analysis regardless of publication consent, provided the footage is not shared outside the coaching team.
