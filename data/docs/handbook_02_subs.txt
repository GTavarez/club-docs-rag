# Subscriptions and Payment

The season subscription covers coaching, pitch hire, league entry, referee fees and one training kit. Subscriptions may be paid in full or across four monthly instalments beginning in September. Families registering more than one player receive a reduction on each additional player's subscription. Hardship arrangements are available and are handled confidentially by the club treasurer; no player is excluded from participation for reasons of cost. Subscriptions are non-refundable after the fourth week of the season except where a player suffers a season-ending injury.
