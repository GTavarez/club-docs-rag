# Registration and Eligibility

Players must register before the first scheduled training session of the season. Registration requires a completed player form, proof of date of birth, and payment of the season subscription or an approved payment plan. A player is not eligible to appear in a competitive fixture until registration is complete and recorded in the club system. Age groups are determined by the player's age on 31 August of the current season year. A player may play up an age group with written approval from both coaches and the age group coordinator; playing down is not permitted under any circumstances.
