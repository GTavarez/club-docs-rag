# Holiday Camp Booking and Refunds

The club runs a five-day holiday camp in the school holidays for players aged six to twelve. Places are booked through the club website and paid for at the time of booking. Camp fees may be paid in full only; instalments are not available for camps. Bookings cancelled at least seven days before the first day of camp are refunded in full. Cancellations made later than seven days before camp are not refunded unless the place can be re-sold. A player who falls ill during camp may be refunded for the unused days on production of a note from a parent or guardian.
