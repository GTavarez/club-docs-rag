# Kit and Equipment

Every registered player receives one training kit at the start of the season. Match kit is issued to the squad by the coach and returned at the end of the season, laundered. Shin pads are mandatory for all training sessions and fixtures without exception; a player without shin pads will not be permitted onto the pitch. Boots must be appropriate to the surface. Jewellery of any kind, including earrings and fitness trackers, must be removed before play.
