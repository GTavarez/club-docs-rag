# Annual Report (Extract)

## Chair's introduction
This was a season of steady growth. The club registered more players than in any previous year, and we fielded teams at every age group from under-seven to under-fifteen. Our thanks go to the coaches and volunteers who made this possible.

## Participation
Registration rose compared with the previous season, driven mainly by the under-nine and under-eleven groups. The under-fifteen group remained the smallest. We trialled a girls-only session on Saturday mornings, which attracted regular attendance and will continue next season.

## Facilities
The shortage of floodlit training slots during the winter months continued to limit what the older groups could do. The committee has asked the council for an additional slot. We also replaced two sets of corner flags and a number of training cones from the equipment budget.

## Volunteers
Volunteer numbers were stable but recruitment remains the main constraint on growth. Three new assistant coaches completed their qualifications during the year. The club reimbursed their course fees as agreed by the committee.

## Finances in brief
Income came mainly from subscriptions and a small number of local sponsors. The largest expenses were pitch hire and league entry. The club ended the year with a modest surplus, which has been set aside for equipment replacement. Full accounts are available from the treasurer on request.

## Looking ahead
Priorities for next season are additional floodlit training, a parents' information evening to recruit volunteers, and a review of the registration process to make it simpler for new families.
