# Coach Handbook (Extract)

## Planning a session
Each session should have a clear focus and last no longer than sixty minutes for the under-nine groups and ninety minutes for older groups. Plan the session in advance and share the plan with your assistant. Arrive thirty minutes before the session to set out equipment and check the pitch for hazards. Check that the first aid kit is present and in date.

## Running a session
Start with a short warm-up. Keep waiting time to a minimum and aim for every player to have a touch of the ball every few seconds. Use small-sided games rather than long queues. Give praise for effort as well as outcomes. Ensure water breaks are taken regularly, especially in warm weather. 

## Behaviour
Coaches are role models. Do not use sarcasm or shout at players. Deal with poor behaviour calmly and, where needed, speak to the parent after the session rather than during it. Report any behaviour that concerns you to the welfare officer.

## Selection and playing time
At the younger age groups every player should receive roughly equal playing time across the season. From under-thirteen upward selection may take into account attendance and effort, as set out in the attendance guidance. Explain selection decisions to players honestly and kindly. 

## After the session
Collect all equipment and count the cones. Record attendance and any injury in the incident log. Tell parents about any minor knocks. Lock the equipment store and return the key to the secretary or the nominated key holder.
