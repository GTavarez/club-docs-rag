# Club Constitution (Extract)

## Name and purpose
The club exists to provide affordable football for children and young people in the local area, to promote participation regardless of ability, and to operate safely and inclusively. The club is a non-profit organisation and any surplus is reinvested in the club. 

## Membership
Membership is open to all players who register in accordance with the registration procedure. Parents and guardians of registered players are associate members and may attend the annual general meeting. Associate members have one vote per family at the annual general meeting. Honorary members may be proposed by the committee in recognition of outstanding service and have no voting rights.

## The committee
The committee consists of the chair, secretary, treasurer, welfare officer, fixtures secretary, equipment officer and up to four ordinary members. Committee members are elected at the annual general meeting for a term of two years and may stand for re-election. A quorum is five committee members including the chair or the secretary. The committee may co-opt additional members to fill a vacancy until the next annual general meeting. A committee member who misses three consecutive meetings without apology may be asked to stand down.

## Finances
The financial year runs from first of July to thirtieth of June. The treasurer presents the annual accounts at the annual general meeting. Any single item of spending above five hundred pounds requires committee approval. The club holds one bank account requiring two signatories. Accounts are examined by an independent examiner who is not a committee member.

## Amendments
The constitution may be amended at an annual general meeting or special general meeting by a two-thirds majority of members present and voting. Notice of a proposed amendment must be given in writing at least twenty-one days before the meeting.
