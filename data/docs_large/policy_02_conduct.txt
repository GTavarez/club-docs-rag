# Code of Conduct

Players, coaches, and spectators are expected to treat match officials with respect at all times. Dissent toward an official is a disciplinary matter for the club regardless of any action taken by the league. Coaches are responsible for the conduct of their spectators. A first incident results in a written warning; a second results in a touchline ban for a period determined by the committee. The club operates a no-tolerance position on discriminatory language, with immediate suspension pending investigation.
