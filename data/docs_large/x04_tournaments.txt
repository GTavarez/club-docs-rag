# Tournament Entry Rules

Tournament entries are made by the age group coordinator, not by individual parents. The entry fee is paid from the club tournament budget. A team entering a tournament must bring a signed team sheet, proof of player ages and a first aid kit. Where a tournament is more than two hours from the club, the committee must approve the trip in advance. Players may not play in more than three matches in one day. Tournament cancellations because of weather are decided by the tournament organiser, not by the club.
