# Privacy Notice

The club holds the personal data that parents provide at registration: names, dates of birth, contact details and emergency contacts. Medical information is held only where a parent has provided it and is shared only with the coach and first aider for that age group. Registration records are kept for six years after a player leaves. A parent may ask for the data held about their child by writing to the club secretary, and the club will respond within one calendar month. Photographs are covered by the separate photo consent policy.
