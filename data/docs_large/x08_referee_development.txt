# Referee Development

The club encourages players aged 14 and over to qualify as youth referees. The club pays the course fee for the first attempt and reimburses travel to the course. Youth referees may officiate matches for age groups at least two years younger than themselves. A youth referee must be accompanied by an adult mentor for their first five matches. Referee fees for club fixtures are set by the league and are not paid to youth referees under 16.
